# Templates, integrations, and offers (overview)

## Templates

**Templates** help standardize **documents** and **workflows** your team reuses across deals—so you’re not rebuilding the same structure every time. Your administrator may maintain a **template library** and field definitions.

## Integrations

**Integrations** is where your workspace connects external systems—calendar, e-sign, messaging providers, or CRM-style links—depending on what your deployment enables. Some integrations are **read-only** at first; others may write data back into Deal Coordinator.

## Offers

**Offers** (inside a **deal**) support comparing and tracking offer-related artifacts. The exact experience depends on brokerage policy and whether offer comparison or acceptance flows are enabled.

---

*If a feature is missing in your environment, it may not yet be turned on for your workspace.*
