# Settings, workspace, and your profile

## Settings

Under **Settings** you can manage **workspace preferences** and **your profile** information that the coordinator and team rely on—contact details, license fields where collected, and related brokerage metadata (as your product admin enables).

Some fields may be required before certain **stages** or **review** steps complete.

## Workspace

Your account can belong to one or more **workspaces** (brokerages or teams). After you sign in, you pick which workspace you’re working in. **Signing out** clears that choice on this device.

## Responsible broker and brokerage data

If your state or brokerage requires a **responsible broker** or similar disclosure on documents, your administrator may maintain that list in settings so it can be used on materials you generate.
