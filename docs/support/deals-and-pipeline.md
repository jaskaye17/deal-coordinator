# Your deals and pipeline

A **deal** is one transaction your brokerage is working through—often a listing or a buyer side—with a **stage** that shows where it is in the process (for example listing active, under contract, or closed).

## Deals list

Open **Deals** in the sidebar to see your workspace’s deals. You can open a deal to see full detail.

## Deal detail

On a deal page you’ll find **tabs** for different angles on the same transaction:

- **Overview** — High-level summary and key facts.  
- **Intake** — Structured fields and confidence—useful when information came from chat or documents.  
- **Documents** — Deal documents you’re tracking in the app.  
- **Deal files** — Folders and file assets (when your team uses structured file storage).  
- **Offers** — Offer-related views (including comparison where enabled).  
- **Tasks** — Things to do for this deal.  
- **Calendar** — Dates tied to the deal.  
- **Communications** — Messages and notes linked to the deal.  
- **Memory** — Longer-lived context the coordinator remembers for this deal.  
- **Exceptions** — Issues that need attention before things move forward.  
- **Audit** — A history of important changes (who did what and when).

Your administrator controls which features are fully rolled out; some tabs may be lighter in early deployments.

## Stages and rules

Moving a deal to a new stage may follow **rules** your brokerage sets—who can advance, and when a **review** is required. If something is blocked, you’ll usually see it in **Exceptions** or **Review Queue**.
