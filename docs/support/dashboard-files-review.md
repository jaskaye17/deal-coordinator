# Dashboard, files, and review queue

## Dashboard

The **Dashboard** is a snapshot of activity in your **workspace**—counts and summaries that help you see what needs attention without opening every deal. Exact metrics depend on how your workspace is configured.

## Files

**Files** is the workspace-wide **library** of folders and file assets (not the same as every attachment inside a single deal). Use it when your team organizes documents across deals or shared areas.  
Inside a **deal**, the **Deal files** tab shows files scoped to that transaction.

## Review queue

**Review Queue** is for work items that need a **reviewer** or **broker** before the deal can proceed—policy checks, quality gates, or risk steps. Open an item to see context and take action.

If your role is **agent**, you may see items returned for more information. If you’re a **reviewer** or **admin**, you may approve or send back items per your brokerage rules.

---

*Your administrator defines review policies and who holds which roles.*
